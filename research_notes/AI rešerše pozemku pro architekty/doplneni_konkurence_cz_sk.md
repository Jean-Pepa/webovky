# Doplnění konkurence CZ/SK: plot-report services and ÚP-regulation tools — verified provider profiles and verdict for architects' pre-design site research (state as of October 2026)

## 1. NEMO Report: what STANDARD and KOMPLET contain, whether there is a developer/architect product, who the company and its partners are, and whether it extracts ÚP regulations

### Takeaway
NEMO Report sells risk reports for the transaction chain: buyers, agents and valuers. It was founded around 2020 by Jan Záhora and belongs to Marek Rosenbaum's EHS group, which also owns Bezrealitky; the year it joined is 2022 or 2023, as sources differ. KOMPLET, the "stavební záměr" tier, adds "podrobné informace a obrazové přílohy z ÚP" to STANDARD's risk summary and LV extract. No source shows it extracting spatial regulations (KZP, max. height or floors, min. plot size), so it shows the plan rather than reading it. NEMO has no dedicated developer or architect product. Architects and projektanti are named only as part of a B2B user base of more than 1 000, served through individual deals and an API.

### Inferences
- NEMO is a **risk-scoring and data-distribution business for the transaction chain**: portals, agents, valuers through NEMExpress, and owner checks through CRIF. It is not a design tool.
- KOMPLET's ÚP part looks like plan excerpts and images put together with human comments. That fits the 2–3 working-day delivery and the "komentáře" item. It does not look like structured extraction of regulations.
- Group ownership gives NEMO protected B2C distribution through Bezrealitky. A product for architects would not compete for that channel head-on.
- NEMO already names architects and projektanti among its B2B users and has an API. That makes it the incumbent best placed to add a "projektant" tier quickly if architect demand became visible.
- The year EHS acquired NEMO is unresolved. It is 2022 if NEMO was reported together with the M&M deal, or 2023 per the Euro.cz summary.

## 2. Other Czech providers: what AIkatastr.cz, Pozemkov.cz, parcelgps.com, TH Prověření pozemku, Rozborpozemku.cz, PozemeCheck, Visidoo, Audit pozemku, Bezvadný pozemek and Nemoinspekt actually deliver, and how

### Takeaway
The providers fall into two groups.
- **Automated:**
  - **AIkatastr** sells a 99 Kč PDF in minutes and markets itself as AI. It claims to give the ÚP code, a verbal description and KZP, though some of its regulation values are generic "orientační" figures. A Pro tier targets makléři, projektanti and investoři. The operator is GRAVITATION & partners s.r.o., Brno.
  - **Pozemkov** is a Palacký University spin-off run by Daturing s.r.o., Olomouc. Its basic report is automated; the "complete" report costs 2 490 Kč and adds a manual expert check and official utility statements.
  - **parcelgps** is only a cadastre API.
- **Manual:** TH, Audit pozemku, PozemeCheck and Bezvadný pozemek explicitly interpret ÚP regulations (height, % zastavění, min. výměra). They charge 899–20 000 Kč, take 48 h to 10 working days, and sell to plot buyers. Nemoinspekt, Visidoo and Rozbor are manual too, but their reports' contents were not retrieved.

## 6. Verdict: which provider, if any, is aimed at architects/designers, and what is missing for pre-design rešerše pozemku

### Takeaway
As of October 2026, no Czech or Slovak provider was found that sells a complete pre-design site-research product built for architects. The prior notes' claim that "none is sold to architects" needs refining, because pieces now exist:
- **Devcheck:** an automated regulation map with KZP/HPP/parking/setback calculators, but only for 4 Czech cities.
- **Jednotka.pro:** automated extraction of the regulation text that applies to a parcel.
- **AIkatastr Pro:** bulk ÚP tools and an AI copilot that names projektanti among its users.
- **NEMO:** architects and projektanti are B2B users through individual deals and an API, but its product (KOMPLET) is a buyer risk report.

Everything else sells to plot buyers and realtors. Missing for architects:
1. **nationwide**, verified and **cited** extraction of numeric regulations

### Inferences
- **Answer to "does the product already exist?": not as a whole, but the pieces are being built now.**
  - The **direct competitors for an architect product** are Devcheck (big cities, regulation map plus feasibility calculators), Jednotka.pro (AI reading of ÚP text) and AIkatastr Pro (AI copilot, bulk ÚP tools, cheap automated PDF).
  - The **distribution-strong incumbent** is NEMO, which already has architects as B2B users, an API and group-owned channels.
  - The **manual experts** (TH, Audit pozemku, PozemeCheck) prove that buyers pay 0.9–20 k Kč for regulation interpretation, and they set the quality bar.
- **What a new architect-oriented product would need in order to stand out:**
  1. Nationwide coverage beyond the large cities: smaller municipalities' ÚPs, where Devcheck does not go.
  2. Numeric regulations (KZP/KZ, height or floors, min. výměra, odstupy, stavební čára, roof, conditional uses) taken from the specific ÚP version. Each item would carry a page or paragraph citation, a confidence level and human verification, unlike AIkatastr's "orientační" values.
  3. All limits in one output: OP, nature (ÚSES/NATURA/CHKO), heritage, geology/radon, flood, terrain (DMR 5G), and the list of utility managers with ready-made vyjádření requests.
  4. Design deliverables: buildable envelope and capacity, CAD/BIM export of the plot and limits, and a white-label report the architect can hand to the client.
  5. A professional workflow: multi-parcel projects, team seats, an API, and alerts on ÚP changes.
- **Risk:** the gap is closing fast. The two most AI-native vendors are young (AIkatastr, Pozemkov), Devcheck and Jednotka.pro already exist, and NEMO could add a "projektant" tier. Positioning should rest on verified nationwide regulation data and architect deliverables, not on "AI plot report", which is already a 99 Kč commodity.

### Gaps
- The verdict depends on **Devcheck and Jednotka.pro**, which were found only in the last search and could not be profiled for operator, price, coverage, accuracy or target segment. Verify them directly, ideally with test accounts.
- No sample reports from any provider were read. "Not stated" in the matrix means only that the search summaries did not mention an item.
- No Czech or Slovak AI-for-ÚP startup news was found in the major startup media. That absence comes from a single restricted search and should not be read as proof.
