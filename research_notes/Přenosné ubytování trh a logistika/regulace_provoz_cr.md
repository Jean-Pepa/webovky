## 1. Permitting under zákon č. 283/2021 Sb.: is a unit a "stavba"; zařízení staveniště, dočasná stavba, mobilheimy; Příloha č. 1 vs č. 2; timelines; territorial planning; does the main project's permit cover a worker camp?

### Takeaway
MMR's reading has five parts:
- A container, module or mobile home placed on a plot for the long term is a stavba, specifically a "výrobek plnící funkci stavby".
- It needs no permit only if it is a drobná stavba (Příloha č. 1). Stavby pro bydlení are excluded from that list.
- Otherwise it is a jednoduchá stavba (Příloha č. 2). That means a povolení záměru with a 30-day statutory decision period and, under § 230(2), no kolaudace unless the building is "pro bydlení a rodinnou rekreaci".
- MMR's May 2025 methodology lists stavby zařízení staveniště, including buňky and kontejnery, in Příloha č. 2, both on and off the staveniště.
- Since 1 Aug 2025, site facilities are drobné (no permit) only when they serve a drobná stavba.

Real permitting most often takes 3–5 months. Local politics is becoming the binding constraint: municipalities around Dukovany are amending their územní plány to block new ubytovny.

**Legal status and recent or pending changes**

- MMR runs a site titled "Nový stavební zákon 2026". This points to further reform work, but the content was not retrieved — [novystavebnizakon.gov.cz](https://novystavebnizakon.gov.cz/)
- ČKAIT published an update on jednoduché stavby "k 1. srpnu 2025", meaning Příloha 1 and Příloha 2 were changed by an amendment effective 1 Aug 2025 — [ČKAIT – Jednoduché stavby, aktualizace k 1. 8. 2025](https://zpravy.ckait.cz/vydani/2025-04/jednoduche-stavby-aktualizace-k-1-srpnu-2025/)
- The new technical decree is vyhláška č. 146/2024 Sb. o požadavcích na výstavbu. According to the search summary, it treats networks serving zařízení staveniště as dočasné stavby — [zakonyprolidi 146/2024](https://www.zakonyprolidi.cz/cs/2024-146)

**Zařízení staveniště (site facilities, including buňky and kontejnery)**
- The MMR methodology "Jednoduché stavby" (22 May 2025) lists stavby zařízení staveniště among jednoduché stavby in Příloha č. 2. It names stavební buňky, kontejnery and storage or handling areas, both inside and outside the staveniště — [MMR Metodika Jednoduché stavby 22. 5. 2025](https://mmr.gov.cz/getattachment/Ministerstvo/Stavebni-pravo/Pravo-a-legislativa/Novy-stavebni-zakon/Metodiky/Stavby-podle-noveho-stavebniho-zakona/Drobne-stavby-podle-zakona-c-283-2021-Sb-(1)/Dokumenty/Jednoduche-stavby-podle-zakona-c-283-2021-Sb/Metodika-Jednoduche-stavby_22_5_2025.pdf.aspx?lang=cs-CZ&ext=.pdf). An earlier version, dated 18 June 2024, is hosted by Olomouc — [Metodika 18. 6. 2024](https://www.olomouc.eu/administrace/repository/gallery/articles/11_/11686/Metodika-Jednoduche-stavby_18_6_2024.cs.pdf)
- According to ČKAIT, since 1 Aug 2025 the stavby zařízení staveniště needed to build or remove a drobná stavba are themselves drobné stavby and need no permit — [ČKAIT 2025](https://zpravy.ckait.cz/vydani/2025-04/jednoduche-stavby-aktualizace-k-1-srpnu-2025/)
- The drobné stavby list also contains an item "m) stavby zařízení staveniště". Some sources simplify this to "all site facilities are permit-free" — [ČKAIT – Drobné stavby podle NSZ](https://zpravy.ckait.cz/vydani/2024-03/drobne-stavby-podle-nsz/). Another snippet placed the item at bod "p)" — [zakonyprolidi 283/2021](https://www.zakonyprolidi.cz/cs/2021-283). **The sources disagree on the point letter and scope; verify against the current Příloha č. 1.**
- Příloha č. 2 (jednoduché stavby) covers "výrobky plnící funkci stavby, včetně základových konstrukcí pro ně, pokud nejde o drobnou stavbu". This item captures stand-alone stavební buňky and kontejnery — [ČKAIT – Jak se člení stavby podle NSZ (PDF)](https://zpravy.ckait.cz/media/2024-3/z-i-ckait-2024-03-priloha-jak-se-cleni-stavby-podle-noveho-stavebniho-zakona.pdf)
- Příloha č. 1 excludes stavby pro bydlení from drobné stavby. For certain drobné stavby a "doplňková funkce bydlení nebo pobytové rekreace" is not allowed — [MMR Metodika Drobné stavby 25. 3. 2025](https://mmr.gov.cz/getattachment/Ministerstvo/Stavebni-pravo/Pravo-a-legislativa/Novy-stavebni-zakon/Metodiky/Stavby-podle-noveho-stavebniho-zakona/Drobne-stavby-podle-zakona-c-283-2021-Sb/Dokumenty/Drobne-stavby-podle-zakona-c-283-2021-Sb/Metodika-Drobne-stavby_25_3_2025.pdf.aspx?ext=.pdf&lang=cs-CZ), [zakonyprolidi 283/2021](https://www.zakonyprolidi.cz/cs/2021-283)
- Under the old law (183/2006 with vyhl. 501/2006, § 24e), stavby zařízení staveniště were permitted as dočasné stavby. Some needed neither ohlášení nor a permit; others needed an ohlášení under § 104(2)(g) — [ČKAIT Profesis A 3.15](https://profesis.ckait.cz/dokumenty-ckait/a-3-15/), [Stavárna](https://www.stavarna.com/download2/633_2579_cs_21_spr_staveniste_a_zarizeni_staveniste.pdf). The builder had to remove them once the main stavba was finished, usually by its kolaudace — [ESTAV – Stavba dočasná](https://www.estav.cz/cz/7338.stavba-docasna-na-jak-dlouho-se-povoluje-z-ceho-ma-byt-postavena-a-kdy-se-odstranuje)
- An older decree allowed only stavby zařízení staveniště and network or road connections to be placed outside the stavební pozemek — [vyhl. 137/1998 (outdated)](https://www.stavebnistandardy.cz/doc/legislativa/zakony/137-98.htm). One search summary attributed the same rule to 283/2021, which is **unverified**.
- In construction-management teaching, site facilities are split into staveništní and mimostaveništní (for example on company premises). A ubytovna is described as an exceptional solution — [VUT Brno FAST – Zařízení staveniště](https://www.fce.vutbr.cz/ekr/asp/AktualityPredmety/FA/11_Zarizeni_staveniste.pdf)

**Mobile homes (mobilheimy) and "výrobek plnící funkci stavby"**
- MMR issued a metodické doporučení "Mobilní domy" covering specification, povolování and kolaudace, updated on 5 Nov 2025 — [MMR – Metodiky](https://mmr.gov.cz/cs/ministerstvo/stavebni-pravo/pravo-a-legislativa/novy-stavebni-zakon/metodiky). It replaced the May 2022 version — [MMR Mobilní domy 5/2022](https://mmr.gov.cz/getattachment/c94419bd-44f5-4a64-8120-3bbc12bcb00f/Mobilni-domy_5-2022.pdf.aspx?lang=cs-CZ&ext=.pdf)
- Blog summaries of the methodology report the following (attribution uncertain) — [tinyhousespace](https://tinyhousespace.com/mobilni-domy-vs-stavebni-urad-dle-metodiky-mmr-2/), [mobilni-domy-prodej.cz](https://www.mobilni-domy-prodej.cz/blog/mobilni-domy-legislativa-2026), [JHRE](https://www.jhre.cz/tiny-house-bez-povoleni-2026):
  - The decisive tests are purpose and long-term or permanent placement on a specific plot.
  - A mobile home placed for living, recreation or business is assessed as a výrobek plnící funkci stavby.
  - A genuinely mobile vehicle operated under vehicle rules need not be a stavba.
  - The methodology describes a mobile home as single-storey, a little over 2 m high, weighing 2–8 t.
  - If it is a drobná stavba under Příloha č. 1, no permit is needed; otherwise a permit is always needed.
- After modifications such as insulation, new windows or an extension, a mobile home is treated as a stavba rather than a product — [davidzahumensky.cz on the MMR methodology](https://www.davidzahumensky.cz/2025/07/18/nova-metodika-mmr-mobilni-domy-a-jejich-povolovani/)
- A mobile home used for housing is not a drobná stavba. It falls among jednoduché stavby, which need a permit — [ČKAIT – Nové požadavky na stavby pro bydlení](https://zpravy.ckait.cz/vydani/2024-03/nove-pozadavky-na-stavby-pro-bydleni/)
- **Conflicting commercial claims:**
  - One site says a product of up to 55 m² and 4 m in height on recreation land needs no permit, while year-round living triggers stricter rules (energy, permanent utilities, hygiene) — [zahradni-domky-vyprodej.cz](https://www.zahradni-domky-vyprodej.cz/mobilheim-celorocni-bydleni-pravidla-kolaudace/)
  - Another says only an "územní souhlas" is needed — [hypotecnispecialista.cz](https://www.hypotecnispecialista.cz/mobilheimy/). That is an instrument of the old law, so treat this claim as unreliable.
- Mobile homes cannot be placed on undeveloped land, meadows or agricultural land, and fines can apply — [JRS reality](https://www.jrsreal.cz/novy-stavebni-zakon-32)

**Kolaudace (§ 230)**
- Under § 230(2), jednoduché stavby need no kolaudační rozhodnutí, "s výjimkou jednoduchých staveb pro bydlení a rodinnou rekreaci" — [krajta § 230](https://krajta.slv.cz/2021/283/par_230). The builder must still notify the stavební úřad of completion "neprodleně". Such a stavba may be used immediately after completion, but only for the purpose stated in the povolení — [krajta § 230](https://krajta.slv.cz/2021/283/par_230)

**Timelines**
- Statutory deadline: the stavební úřad decides within 30 days for a jednoduchá stavba and 60 days for other stavby. It may extend these by up to 30 and 60 days respectively — [Mobis](https://mobis.cz/projektova-dokumentace/pruvodce/jak-dlouho-trva-povoleni-stavby) (cites § 196). MMR's dashboard cites **§ 195** and "30 + 30 dnů", so the **paragraph number conflicts** between sources — [MMR dashboard](https://mmr.gov.cz/MMR/media/MMR-MMR/Dashboard%20stavebn%C3%ADch%20%C5%99%C3%ADzen%C3%AD/index.html)
- The MMR dashboard tracks proceedings opened since 1 Jul 2024 at 651 stavební úřady and includes a "víc než rok" (more than a year) bucket for proceedings over 365 days — [MMR dashboard](https://mmr.gov.cz/MMR/media/MMR-MMR/Dashboard%20stavebn%C3%ADch%20%C5%99%C3%ADzen%C3%AD/index.html)
- The 2026 ČKAIT survey found that the most common time from application to a final permit was 3–5 months — [ČKAIT PIK 2026 (PDF)](https://zpravy.ckait.cz/media/pdf/pik-2026-delka-povolovani-staveb-v-cr.pdf). ČKAIT's headline says only 5% of stavby are not permitted within 18 months — [ČKAIT 2026-02](https://zpravy.ckait.cz/vydani/2026-02/jen-5-staveb-se-nepovoli-do-roka-a-pul-vyplyva-ze-dvou-pruzkumu-ckait/)

- A precedent: a container camp for about 6,000 workers was built at the Orlen refinery in Płock, Poland — [Ekonomický deník](https://ekonomickydenik.cz/jaderne-staveniste-dukovany-muze-to-byt-drsne-ukazuje-priklad-stavby-nove-rafinerie-v-polskem-plocku/)

### Inferences
- **Camp on the construction site.** The defensible classification is stavba zařízení staveniště, which makes it a jednoduchá stavba. It can be permitted inside the main project's povolení záměru, if the camp is part of that documentation and the site-organisation design (ZOV). Otherwise it needs its own simplified povolení. No retrieved source says expressly that the main permit covers it. Under the old law, such camps were routinely handled as a dočasná stavba tied to completion of the main works.
- **Camp off the construction site.** MMR's methodology expressly allows zařízení staveniště off site. Even so, a camp serving several projects or lasting several years risks being treated as a stavba ubytovacího zařízení. That would mean full PBŘ and hygiene documentation, a check against the územní plán and possibly kolaudace.
- **Whether kolaudace applies.** It turns on whether a worker dormitory counts as "bydlení" (kolaudace required even for a jednoduchá stavba) or as "ubytování". No retrieved source settles this, and it is a material legal-risk item for anyone selling camps.
- **Where the time actually goes.** The 30-day decision clock is not the bottleneck. The time goes on the pre-application chain: site search, landowner, územní plán, the HZS, KHS and vodoprávní stanoviska, distribution-operator statements, and municipal consent.
- **Gap for a service company.** Type-approved module layouts could come with a reusable PBŘ, hygiene documents and a jednoduchá stavba application pack. A "site bank" of pre-cleared plots would complement them; Dukovany alone has 41 plots on offer.

### Gaps
- The exact current text and point letters of Příloha č. 1 and č. 2, after the 1 Aug 2025 amendment and the June 2026 consolidation. Fetches failed.
- The paragraph defining "dočasná stavba" in 283/2021, how the time limit is set and when removal is due.
- Explicit MMR or court guidance on whether the main project's povolení záměru covers on-site or off-site worker camps.
- Real average durations for jednoduché stavby; the MMR dashboard figures were not retrieved.
- What the "Nový stavební zákon 2026" reform contains.
- Conformity requirements for modules as stavební výrobky (prohlášení o vlastnostech), not researched.

## 2. Fire safety: požárně bezpečnostní řešení (PBŘ), distances between modules, ČSN norms for accommodation, escape routes, detectors

### Takeaway
Fire design for accommodation follows ČSN 73 0833 (budovy pro bydlení a ubytování, OB groups). Separation distances (odstupové vzdálenosti) are calculated per facade, from the largest openings and the fire load. Vyhláška 23/2008 Sb. explicitly requires autonomous smoke detection (zařízení autonomní detekce a signalizace) in every sleeping room and on the escape route of construction-site accommodation. Accommodation with more than 75 beds also needs a public-address system (rozhlas s nuceným poslechem). Container-specific spacing tables and extinguisher counts were not found.

### Cited Findings
- ČSN 73 0833 covers the design of buildings for bydlení and ubytování. It also covers changes of use that convert other spaces into housing or accommodation — [FA ČVUT lecture](https://www.fa.cvut.cz/fakulta/ustavy/15118-ustav-nauky-o-budovach/nsii/ah03-prednasky-architekti.pdf), [HZS ČR – Poskytování ubytovacích služeb ve stavbách určených k bydlení](https://hzscr.gov.cz/soubor/poskytovani-ubytovacich-sluzeb-ve-stavbach-urcenych-k-bydleni-pdf.aspx) (attribution uncertain; HZS document content not retrieved)
- Project PBŘs found in search use the OB groups:
  - One defines OB3 as "budovy pro ubytování o projektované ubytovací kapacitě nejvýše 75 osob umístěných do 3. nadzemního podlaží".
  - Another treats each dwelling cell as its own požární úsek (OB2).
  - A PBŘ for a container-type accommodation block makes each two-bed room, with its shared sanitary facilities, a separate požární úsek ("PÚ N 301 až PÚ N 318").
  - Sources: [PBŘ VŠTE](https://is.vstecb.cz/th/m2q8p/D.1.3_Pozarne_-_bezpecnostni_reseni.pdf), [PBŘ ÚP ČR (MPSV tender)](https://mpsv.ezak.cz/document_7639/12565ac4b4e3cf000-pozarni-zprava-pdf), [PBŘ Kozlí](http://www.address.cz/data/www.obeckozli.cz/files/III.1_RP_lokality_Z9_Barandovska_zatoka_PBRS_text.pdf) (which quote comes from which document is uncertain; check the OB definitions against the standard itself)
- Odstupové vzdálenosti are set from the largest opening on each side, the length and height of the požární úsek, the percentage of požárně otevřená plocha and the požární riziko. A worked family-house example gives 2.3 m straight ahead and 1.3 m to the sides, using the limiting heat-flux density — [ČVUT PBŘ RD](https://tzb.fsv.cvut.cz/files/vyuka/125ypbz/cviceni/1_uloha/Pozarne_bezpecnostni_reseni_RD.pdf)
- Vyhláška 23/2008 Sb. o technických podmínkách požární ochrany staveb sets the following (paragraph number not confirmed from snippets):
  - An ubytovací zařízení that is not required to have EPS must have zařízení autonomní detekce a signalizace in every accommodation room. The same applies to common areas (except those with no fire risk) and to the route to the exit, unless that route is a chráněná úniková cesta.
  - Capacity above 75 → rozhlas s nuceným poslechem.
  - Sleeping in a building that is not an ubytovací zařízení → a separate požární úsek with at most 20 beds, plus autonomous detection.
  - **"Stavba ubytovacího zařízení zařízení staveniště"** must have autonomous detection in each room used for accommodation and on the escape route.
  - Devices must meet ČSN EN 14604 (smoke alarms) or the ČSN EN 54 series.
  - Sources: [TZB-info – hlásiče dle 23/2008](https://www.tzb-info.cz/elektricka-pozarni-signalizace/4835-pouziti-hlasicu-pozaru-podle-nove-vyhlasky-c-23-2008-sb-o-technickych-podminkach-pozarni-ochrany-staveb), [23/2008 consolidated text 2022 (PDF)](https://www.zakonyprolidi.cz/media2/file/2205/File51388.pdf?attachment-filename=7384134-2022-05-03-zneni-se-zmenami-7384156.pdf), [zakonyprolidi 23/2008](https://www.zakonyprolidi.cz/cs/2008-23)
- The decree covers design, construction and use conditions generally, not only detectors and portable extinguishers. Specific extinguisher requirements for accommodation did not appear in the snippets — [zalitobetonem.cz](https://zalitobetonem.cz/blog/set-ke-kolaudaci-staveb-a2-hasici-pristroj-detektor/), [HZS MSK – vyhl. 23/2008](http://www.hzsmsk.cz/prevence/vyhl23.php)
- Firefighters take part in police-led inspections of ubytovny and check sites "po linii požární ochrany" (for fire protection). Example: Rokycany — [Policie ČR – Kontrola pobytu cizinců](https://policie.gov.cz/clanek/uzemni-utvary-sprava-zapadoceskeho-kraje-zpravodajstvi-kontrola-pobytu-cizincu.aspx)

### Inferences
- Containers designed as many small požární úseky keep the fire concept simple. The separation distances between module rows then set site density and layout. A pre-calculated PBŘ "kit" for each module type and layout pattern could be reused across projects.
- Every sleeping room must have an autonomous detector, so a camp of 100 rooms has at least 100 devices to test, log and re-battery. This suits device inventory and maintenance logging (IoT or app) — a natural BULO feature.
- The fire classification of the module's construction (mineral wool versus PUR/EPS sandwich cores) probably drives the separation distances. This is unsourced; see Gaps.

### Gaps
- Not found: requirements for portable extinguishers (PHP), EPS thresholds for camps, escape-route lengths for container layouts, and reaction-to-fire classes of sandwich panels.
- Not found: HZS practice on minimum spacing between modules, and whether an HZS závazné stanovisko is needed for a jednoduchá stavba camp.
- Not found: periodic inspection duties for detectors and extinguishers (the operating-phase fire-prevention rules).
- The HZS ČR guidance document was found but its content was not retrieved.

## 3. Hygiene requirements for accommodation services (the MZd decree?), minimum space per person, sanitary ratios

### Takeaway
No MZd decree on accommodation services was found. Vyhláška 137/2004 Sb. covers food services (relevant for camp canteens), not accommodation. Accommodation hygiene is governed directly by **§ 21a of zákon č. 258/2000 Sb.**: a provozní řád must be approved by the KHS before operation starts, and it must include linen handling. Since 2015 it must also describe units used for long stays. No binding Czech minimum of m² per bed and no binding sanitary ratio were found; only a non-binding categorisation recommendation of 5–6 m² per bed.

### Cited Findings
- Vyhláška 137/2004 Sb. covers hygienic requirements for stravovací služby and epidemiologically significant activities, not ubytovací služby — [zakonyprolidi 137/2004](https://www.zakonyprolidi.cz/cs/2004-137)
- Under § 21a of zákon 258/2000 Sb., the accommodation operator must draw up a provozní řád, which covers among other things "zacházení s prádlem". It must be submitted to the public-health authority for approval before operation starts, and operation cannot start without it — [HS hl. m. Prahy](https://www.hygpraha.cz/jake-povinnosti-musi-splnit-osoba-poskytujici-ubytovani-v-ubytovacich-zarizenich/), [podnikatel.cz – zákon 258/2000, Díl 4](https://www.podnikatel.cz/zakony/zakon-c-258-2000-sb-o-ochrane-verejneho-zdravi/f2067137/)
- Since 1 Jan 2015, the provozní řád must also state the number and floor area of units used for stays of more than 2 months within 6 months, and how each unit is equipped. This clause targets long-stay hostels such as worker ubytovny — [podnikatel.cz](https://www.podnikatel.cz/zakony/zakon-c-258-2000-sb-o-ochrane-verejneho-zdravi/f2067137/), [KHS Ústí](https://old.khsusti.cz/php/kousky/aktual/20150414/1/clanek.htm) (attribution uncertain)
- The KHS Plzeň template provozní řád says linen is changed after each guest, otherwise as needed and **at least every 14 days**. This is a template recommendation, not a confirmed statutory rule — [KHS Plzeň – vzor provozního řádu](https://www.khsplzen.cz/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/PR-ubytovaciho-zarizeni-aktualizace-2015-1.doc)
- Vyhláška 410/2005 Sb., with its linen intervals, applies to schools and children's facilities, not to general accommodation — [KHS Ostrava – 410/2005](https://www.khsova.cz/docs/01_legislativa/files/410_2005_20230502.pdf)
- The non-binding categorisation recommendation for ubytování v soukromí, kempy, chatové osady and turistické ubytovny gives a minimum net floor area per bed of **5 m² (category \*) and 6 m² (\*\*)**, and **9.5 m² / 10 m²** where bunk beds are used. It is unclear whether the bunk figures are per room or per bed pair, and the document may be outdated — [cestovni-ruch.cz – Doporučení](https://www.cestovni-ruch.cz/kategorizace/doporuceni2.htm)

### Inferences
- In practice the hygiene "permit" for a camp is the KHS-approved provozní řád. It is a semi-standard document, and its linen and cleaning regime then has to be kept and evidenced. Templating the document and scheduling and logging the regime are both automatable.
- Because no binding m² per person or WC ratio exists, density is set by contract, fire limits (for example 20 beds per požární úsek outside accommodation buildings, see §2) and reputation (the Dukovany backlash, §1). This is where a published standard of your own (IFC-like, see §4) could differentiate an offering.

### Gaps
- Binding sanitary ratios (WC, washbasins and showers per person) for ubytovací zařízení. They may sit in vyhláška 146/2024 Sb. or in ČSN standards; not retrieved.
- Whether § 21a applies when an employer houses its own staff free of charge, i.e. when it is not providing a "služba".
- Drinking-water rules for tank-supplied camps (duties for "jiný zdroj pitné vody" and testing); that search could not be run.
- The RILSA "Desatero BOZP ubytovacích zařízení" exists but was not retrieved — [RILSA](https://zsbozp.rilsa.cz/desatero-bozp-ubytovacich-zarizeni)

## 4. Worker-accommodation rules in Czechia (2023–2026 agency changes, SÚIP practice, MPSV standards) and the IFC/EBRD 2009 benchmark

### Takeaway
No Czech statute or decree setting a standard for worker or agency-worker accommodation was found for 2023–2026. Protection is indirect:
- equal conditions for agency workers (§ 309 ZP);
- a ban on deductions in favour of the agency or user (§ 58(3) of zákon 435/2004);
- accommodation charges deductible from pay only under a written dohoda o srážkách (§ 146 ZP);
- frequent police-led joint inspections in which the OIP and HZS take part.

The IFC/EBRD note "Workers' accommodation: processes and standards" (2009) is the de-facto benchmark for large internationally financed or EPC projects. Its main figures are about 4 m² per person, a 2.10 m minimum ceiling height and 2–8 workers per room. It also sets 1 toilet, 1 washbasin, 1 urinal and 1 shower or bath per 15 male workers.

### Cited Findings
**Czech labour-law framework**
- In April 2023 the government approved an amendment to zákon o zaměstnanosti. It limits an agency worker's assignment to one user to 3 years within 5 years and protects agency workers in employer insolvency — [MPSV press release 26 Apr 2023](https://mpsv.gov.cz/cms/documents/edc9a8a9-a06b-b66d-6845-7368266fb8f7/TZ_novela_zakon_o_zamestnanosti_26042023.pdf), [ManpowerGroup](https://www.manpowergroup.cz/novela-zakona-o-zamestnanosti/). MPSV describes changes to agency placement effective 1 Jan 2024 — [MPSV – Změny v oblasti agenturního zaměstnávání](https://mpsv.gov.cz/zmeny-v-oblasti-agenturniho-zamestnavani)
- **Conflict:** a "Zákoník práce 2026" text still states that an agency may not assign the same worker to the same user for more than 12 consecutive calendar months (§ 309) — [Pracomat – § 307a–309](https://www.pracomat.cz/poradna/zakonik-prace/183-zakonik-prace-cast-13-hlava-5.html). What was finally enacted is unclear.
- MPSV has also presented a reworked draft of a whole new zákon o zaměstnanosti. Its status was not retrieved — [Rowan Legal](https://rowan.legal/aktualne/mpsv-predstavilo-prepracovany-navrh-zakona-o-zamestnanosti/)
- Under § 309 ZP, the agency and the user must ensure that an assigned worker's working and pay conditions are no worse than those of a comparable employee of the user — [Pracomat](https://www.pracomat.cz/poradna/zakonik-prace/183-zakonik-prace-cast-13-hlava-5.html)
- Under § 58(3) of zákon 435/2004, deductions from pay for the benefit of the agency or user are prohibited, and placement costs may not be charged to the worker — [ČMKOS study on agency work (2024)](https://www.cmkos.cz/hotothur/2024/04/Agenturni-zamestnavani-studie.pdf) (attribution likely, not certain)
- Accommodation and transport may be deducted from pay only with the worker's written agreement and a known amount. Deductions are allowed only where the law provides or under a dohoda o srážkách ze mzdy (§ 146 ff. ZP) — [Dostupný advokát](https://dostupnyadvokat.cz/blog/co-muze-zamestnavatel-strhnout-z-platu), [GLWA](https://glwa.cz/blog/prace-pres-agenturu-prava/). See also a Q&A on deductions for accommodation of dočasně přidělení zaměstnanci — [Práce a mzda](https://www.praceamzda.cz/otazky/1687/srazky-ze-mzdy-ubytovani-docasne-pridelenych-zamestnancu)
- SÚIP Metodický pokyn 2/2016 on comparable conditions for agency workers is not legally binding — [ČMKOS study](https://www.cmkos.cz/hotothur/2024/04/Agenturni-zamestnavani-studie.pdf), [SP ČR analysis](https://www.spcr.cz/images/Anal%C3%BDza_KA5_Agenturn%C3%AD_zam%C4%9Bstn%C3%A1v%C3%A1n%C3%AD_1.pdf) (attribution uncertain)
- Labour inspectors can fine wage offences but generally cannot recover unpaid wages for the worker — [Dostupný advokát – inspektorát práce](https://dostupnyadvokat.cz/blog/inspektorat-prace)
- A headline reports that the labour inspection will stop accepting anonymous complaints ("Anonymy už přijímat nebudeme, varuje Juchelka"). Only the headline was retrieved, so this is a pending change in SÚIP practice to verify — [Seznam Zprávy](https://www.seznamzpravy.cz/clanek/radce-stiznosti-na-zamestnavatele-vyrazne-pribyva-juchelka-proto-chysta-omezeni-302637)
- The 2026 minimum wage is 22,400 Kč per month, or 134.40 Kč per hour. This is the affordability context for accommodation deductions — [MPSV leaflet 2026](https://mpsv.gov.cz/cms/documents/22a4c972-ceb3-8605-5a8f-2174df433e54/Informa%C4%8Dn%C3%AD%20let%C3%A1k%20o%20minim%C3%A1ln%C3%AD%20mzd%C4%9B%20v%20roce%202026.pdf)

**Enforcement practice (inspections)**
- In the Královéhradecký kraj, police carried out **1,252 checks of accommodation facilities between January and June 2026**, about 200 a month — [orlicky.net](https://www.orlicky.net/clanek.php?id_zpravy=12632122891786261006), [Jičínský deník](https://jicinsky.denik.cz/kraj-a-okoli/vice-kontrol-ubytoven-i-firem-cizinecka-policie-je-letos-vyrazne-aktivnejsi/)

See also the police "Kontroly ubytoven" page — [Policie ČR – Severočeský kraj](https://policie.gov.cz/clanek/uzemni-utvary-sprava-severoceskeho-kraje-zpravodajstvi-kontroly-ubytoven.aspx)
- No KHS involvement and no agency-specific accommodation findings appeared in the inspection coverage found (search-summary observation) — [orlicky.net](https://www.orlicky.net/clanek.php?id_zpravy=12632122891786261006)

**International benchmark: IFC/EBRD (2009) and related guidance**
- The joint IFC/EBRD guidance note dates from August 2009 and runs to about 38 pages. It is aimed at IFC and EBRD specialists, consultants and clients. It covers management and staff, charging fees for accommodation and services, health and safety, security, workers' rights, grievance mechanisms and community relations, and ends with an annex checklist — [IFC](https://www.ifc.org/en/insights-reports/2000/publications-gpn-workersaccommodation), [World Bank copy (PDF)](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/604561468170043490/pdf/602530WP0worke10Box358316B01PUBLIC1.pdf)
- Figures from the note — [EBRD PDF](https://www.ebrd.com/downloads/about/sustainability/Workers_accomodation.pdf) (figures from a garbled extraction; verify against the PDF):
  - **1 toilet, 1 hand-wash basin, 1 urinal and 1 bathroom with bench per 15 male workers**;
  - sleeping rooms **about 4 m² per person**, assuming a 2.4 m height; a 3 m² figure also appears, apparently for locker or cupboard space;
  - **minimum ceiling height 2.10 m**;
  - benchmark standards range from **2 to 8 workers per room**.
- EBRD's PR2 toolkit gives good international practice as **10–12.5 m³ (volume) or 4–5.5 m² (floor area) per resident** — [EBRD PR2 Toolkit](https://www.ebrd.com/content/dam/ebrd_dxp/assets/pdfs/environment---sustainability/implement-performance-requirements/prs/pr2/PR2-Toolkit-EBRD-clients-labour-and-working-condition.pdf)
- EBRD PR4 guidance: sanitary facilities must never be shared by male and female workers, with separate facilities for women where needed — [EBRD PR4 Guidance Note](https://www.ebrd.com/documents/environment/guidance-note-performance-requirement-4-health-safety-and-security.pdf). The EBRD briefing note BN05 (July 2021) says accommodation and welfare facilities should include toilets with handwashing, clean drinking water and fire-prevention equipment — [EBRD BN05](https://www.ebrd.com/content/dam/ebrd_dxp/assets/pdfs/environment---sustainability/implement-performance-requirements/prs/pr4/Briefing-note-BN05-Setting-up-a-safe-site-English.pdf)
- Other benchmarks, not IFC/EBRD:
  - an IOM checklist sets at least 1 toilet, 1 washbasin and 1 tub or shower per 6 persons — [IOM checklist](https://publications.iom.int/system/files/pdf/MWG-Tool-5-Accomodation-checklist_0.pdf);
  - a US camp guide uses 1 toilet per 15 people and urinals per 25 men — [CDPH blog](https://www.cdph.net/blog/worker-accommodation-camp).

### Inferences
- Without a Czech worker-housing standard, the legal floor is the general building, fire and hygiene rules plus labour law. For lender-financed or international EPC projects such as Dukovany II (KHNP), IFC/EBRD-type standards will probably be imposed by contract. That creates demand for camps whose density, sanitary ratios, fee transparency and grievance handling can be audited.
- Deductions for accommodation are a recurring source of disputes and inspection findings for agencies. Software that produces signed dohody o srážkách, transparent per-bed fee statements and damage records with photos reduces that risk.
- With about 200 police checks a month in a single region, occupancy records (who sleeps in which bed, with which ID) need to be accurate in real time and exportable on demand.

## 5. Reporting and administration: Ubyport, evidence ubytovaných and domovní kniha, poplatek z pobytu, GDPR, trade licence, eTurista

### Takeaway
An operator housing foreign workers carries three overlapping duties:
1. **Ubyport.** Each foreigner must be reported within **3 working days**. The duty applies to anyone who houses people for payment *or* houses more than 5 foreigners, so it also covers an employer housing its own staff free of charge.
2. **Domovní kniha (register of foreigners).** It must be kept for **6 years** after the last entry.
3. **Evidenční kniha for poplatek z pobytu.** It must also be kept for 6 years. The poplatek applies to paid stays of **up to 60 consecutive days**; exemptions vary by obecně závazná vyhláška (municipal decree).

**eTurista** would merge these duties and cut the foreigner-reporting deadline to 24 hours, but it is not in force. MMR says 2027 with no fixed date; one tracker says 1 Jan 2028.

### Cited Findings
**Ubyport (zákon 326/1999 Sb.)**
- "Ubytovatel je povinen oznámit ubytování cizince do 3 pracovních dnů po jeho ubytování; oznámení učiní útvaru policie" (§ 102) — [MZV – full text of 326/1999 (PDF)](https://mzv.gov.cz/file/335740/Zakon_c._329_1999_Sb..pdf), [Portál veřejné správy](https://portal.gov.cz/sluzby-vs/hlaseni-mista-pobytu-na-uzemi-ubytovatelem-S11845)
- Under § 99(1), an ubytovatel is "každý, kdo poskytuje ubytování za úhradu nebo kdo ubytovává více než 5 cizinců". Day one of the 3-day period is the day accommodation is provided — [Obec Bukovany – Ubytovatel a jeho povinnosti](https://www.obecbukovany.eu/file.php?nid=17681&oid=8891815)
- Businesses report electronically via UBYPORT. Non-businesses may use a paper form at the cizinecká policie office — [Obec Bukovany](https://www.obecbukovany.eu/file.php?nid=17681&oid=8891815)
- **Scope nuance:** the portal's service page speaks of "cizinců, občanů třetích zemí" (third-country nationals), while § 102 speaks of "cizince" generally. Check whether EU citizens must be reported — [Portál veřejné správy](https://portal.gov.cz/sluzby-vs/hlaseni-mista-pobytu-na-uzemi-ubytovatelem-S11845)
- The fine is reported as up to 50,000 Kč. This comes from a single commercial blog and is unverified — [flatly.cz](https://flatly.cz/blog/ubyport-hlaseni-cizincu)
- Ubyport is the Police web application for this reporting. The duty comes from § 100 písm. c) of 326/1999 — [Policie ČR – Co to je UbyPort](https://policie.gov.cz/reditelstvi-sluzby-cizinecke-policie/co-to-je-ubyport). There are three submission modes: a manual web form, upload of a **UNL file**, or **automated submission through a web service** — [Policie ČR – možnosti zasílání dat](https://policie.gov.cz/clanek/jake-jsou-moznosti-zasilani-dat-elektronicky-prostrednictvim-aplikace-ubyport.aspx)
- Commercial sources say the web service is SOAP-based and needs special access granted by the police — [vezpa.it](https://vezpa.it/cz/blog/ubyport-automaticke-hlaseni/), [checkin.blue](https://checkin.blue/blog/ubyport-tri-varianty-hlaseni). A hotel-software vendor markets "Ubyport 2.0" compliance; whether there is an official "2.0" is unverified — [Better Hotel](https://better-hotel.com/en/blog/ubyport-2-0-plneni-zakonnych-povinnosti-zvladnete-s-better-hotelem-snadno-a-bez-starosti/)
- e-Sbírka lists 326/1999 as "12. 6. 2026, aktuální znění", so the Act was amended recently. Whether §§ 99–102 changed is not verified — [e-Sbírka 326/1999](https://e-sbirka.gov.cz/sb/1999/326)

**Domovní kniha / evidence of foreigners and GDPR**
- A 2010 inspection by the ÚOOÚ (data protection authority) cites § 101 of 326/1999 as the basis for keeping the evidenční kniha of accommodated foreigners and for keeping it **6 years** — [ÚOOÚ – Kontrola zpracování osobních údajů hotelových hostů (2010)](https://uoou.gov.cz/cinnost/ochrana-osobnich-udaju/ukoncene-kontroly/kontroly-za-rok-2010/kontrola-zpracovani-osobnich-udaju-hotelovych-hostu). Paragraph numbering may have changed since. Practitioner guides also state 6 years from the last entry — [ubytovacka.cz – Domovní kniha](https://www.ubytovacka.cz/domovni-kniha/), [Chekin – povinnosti ubytovatele](https://chekin.com/cs/blog/povinnosti-ubytovatele/)

**Poplatek z pobytu (zákon 565/1990 Sb.)**
- Under § 3a, the poplatek is charged on a paid stay ("úplatný pobyt") of **at most 60 consecutive calendar days** with one provider. Longer stays fall outside it — [chalupario.cz](https://www.chalupario.cz/prispevky/poplatek-z-pobytu-osvobozeni-odvod)
- Under § 3b(1)(e), people doing seasonal work in the municipality for a legal entity or podnikající fyzická osoba are exempt. Per the MF, this applies only to short stays (under 60 days) of work that recurs regularly with the seasons — [MF MP/02/2020](https://mf.gov.cz/cs/dane-a-ucetnictvi/dane/mistni-spravni-a-soudni-poplatky/odpovedi-na-dotazy/2020/mp-02-2020--poplatek-z-pobytu-osvobozeni-37122), [krajta § 3b](https://krajta.slv.cz/1990/565/par_3b-odst_2)
- Municipalities may add exemptions in their OZV, so the rules differ from town to town — [chalupario.cz](https://www.chalupario.cz/prispevky/poplatek-z-pobytu-osvobozeni-odvod), [MV – výklad k OZV o poplatku z pobytu](https://mv.gov.cz/odk2/soubor/mm-28-04a-pravni-vyklad-pobyt.aspx). Example: Praha 1 exempts state employees only when they stay in state- or municipally-owned facilities — [Praha 1](https://www.praha1.cz/potrebuji-si-vyridit/odbory/poplatek-z-pobytu/)
- The evidenční kniha for the poplatek is kept for 6 years under § 3g(4) — [airbo.cz](https://airbo.cz/navody/poplatek-z-pobytu-a-ubytovaci-kniha/), [ubytovacka.cz – Ubytovací kniha](https://www.ubytovacka.cz/ubytovaci-kniha/) (which of these cites § 3g(4) is uncertain). It records the length and purpose of stay, name, permanent address and ID or passport number.

**eTurista (pending)**
- It is not in force. The e-turista.cz tracker gives a proposed effective date of **1 Jan 2028**, still subject to the legislative process — [e-turista.cz](https://www.e-turista.cz/). In August 2026, MMR presented a plan to launch it **in 2027**, with no date fixed — [ubytovacka.cz – eTurista](https://www.ubytovacka.cz/eturista/). Sources that say mid-2026 are outdated — [BusinessInfo](https://www.businessinfo.cz/clanky/eturista-digitalni-registr-hostu-je-potreba-revoluce-v-kratkodobem-ubytovani-se-ale-odklada/)
- The aim is a single entry point that replaces UbyPort and paper books and forwards data to cizinecká policie, finanční správa and the municipality. The draft **cuts the foreigner-reporting deadline from 3 working days to 24 hours**; this is a proposal and may change — [Chekin – eTurista a konec UbyPortu](https://chekin.com/cs/blog/ubyport-a-e-turista-jak-splnit-povinnosti-evidence-hostu/), [e-turista.cz](https://www.e-turista.cz/)
- Until eTurista launches, the current regime continues: an evidenční kniha for guests plus Ubyport for foreigners — [ubytovacka.cz](https://www.ubytovacka.cz/eturista/)

### Inferences
- **Rotations multiply reportable events.** Each arrival of a foreign worker is an Ubyport report, a domovní kniha entry and a poplatek check (stay ≤60 days? exemption under the local OZV?). As an illustration, 200 foreign workers on 3-week rotations generate roughly 200–300 reports a month.
- **Free employer accommodation.** If the employer houses its own staff without charge, the stay may not be "úplatný", so the poplatek would not apply. The Ubyport duty still applies above 5 foreigners. This is an inference; confirm with the MF or the municipality.
- **eTurista's 24-hour rule.** It would make manual reporting at a camp scale impractical. Integrating with the existing Ubyport web service now, with a later eTurista adapter, is a defensible product wedge for BULO-type software.
- **GDPR.** The records rest on legal obligations, which is a GDPR Art. 6(1)(c) basis. Retention of 6 years followed by deletion, plus access logging, should be automated (inference).

### Gaps
- Not retrieved: GDPR and ÚOOÚ position on scanning or copying passports at check-in.
- Not retrieved: the trade-licence regime for accommodation services (živnost, obor) and whether an employer housing its own staff needs one.
- Not retrieved: the current maximum rate of poplatek z pobytu.
- Not retrieved: MF guidance on employer-paid versus free stays.
- The Ubyport fine has only a single unverified source.

## 6. Utilities: grid connection vs generators vs solar/battery; water tanks vs connection; mobile ČOV and its permits; waste; indicative costs and lead times

### Takeaway
**Grid power** comes slowly and on uncertain timing. Under vyhláška 16/2016 Sb. the applicant pays a podíl na oprávněných nákladech, computed as měrný podíl × reserved capacity, with 50% paid up front. In sample ČEZ Distribuce contracts, the distributor then has **up to 11 months** from that payment to build the connection.

**Wastewater** became harder on **1 Jul 2024**. The simplified ohlášení route for small ČOV ended, so a ČOV now needs a full vodoprávní permit, typically valid for up to 10 years and carrying servicing and sampling conditions.

**Container rental itself is cheap.** An obytná buňka costs about 3,100–3,300 Kč a month and a sanitary container 8,400–11,000 Kč a month, both ex VAT.

No sourced data could be gathered on generators, solar and battery containers, water tanks or waste; the search budget ran out.

### Cited Findings
**Electricity: grid connection (vyhláška ERÚ 16/2016 Sb.)**
- The connection charge is the product of the měrný podíl in Příloha č. 8 and the requested rezervovaný příkon or výkon — [zakonyprolidi 16/2016](https://www.zakonyprolidi.cz/cs/2016-16)
- The applicant pays a **záloha of 50% of the podíl**, capped at 50 million Kč. In the 2022 wording it is due **within 15 days** of signing the smlouva o připojení or the smlouva o smlouvě budoucí — [zakonyprolidi 16/2016](https://www.zakonyprolidi.cz/cs/2016-16), [Sagit (2022 version)](https://sagit.cz/info/uztxt.asp?cd=5&typ=r&det=&levelid=923999&datumakt=1.1.2022&full=y)
- At VN and higher voltage levels, the non-refundable part is 30% if the reservation lasted less than 12 months and 50% otherwise — [krajta § 12(4)](https://krajta.slv.cz/2016/16/par_12-odst_4)
- e-Sbírka labels the 16/2016 text in force from 1 Oct 2024 to 30 Sep 2025 as "minulé znění". A newer version has therefore applied **since 1 Oct 2025**; its content was not retrieved — [e-Sbírka 16/2016](https://e-sbirka.gov.cz/sb/2016/16/2024-10-01?f=16%2F2016&zalozka=text), [EnviWeb – amending decree](https://www.enviweb.cz/128862)
- Sample ČEZ Distribuce connection contracts in public registers (individual cases, not a standard) — [PMDP contract](https://zakazky.pmdp.cz/document_audit_13221/priloze-c-9_smlouva-o-pripojeni-odberneho-mista-a-vyrobniho-zarizeni-25_sop_06_4122451737-002-pdf), [smlouvy.gov.cz – ČEZ Distribuce](https://smlouvy.gov.cz/smlouva/soubor/9572075/Smlouva%20%C4%8CEZ%20Distribuce-anon.pdf) (which term is from which contract is uncertain):
  - the customer pays at least half the podíl within 15 days of signing;
  - the distributor's deadline is **11 months from that payment**, and the customer's own works deadline was **18 months** in one case;
  - if the podíl is not paid even within a one-month grace period, the connection duty and the capacity reservation lapse.

**Wastewater: ČOV (vodní zákon 254/2001 Sb.)**
- A ČOV is a vodní dílo. It needs a vodoprávní permit for discharge (from the obecní or městský úřad, usually its environment department) in addition to building approval. The permit is needed whether the building is used permanently or recreationally — [ABplast – legislation guide](https://www.abplast.cz/dotace-a-legislativa/legislativa-k-domovnim-cistirnam-odpadnich-vod-pruvodce-povolenim-krok-za-krokem)
- **The simplified ohlášení route for domestic ČOV ended on 1 Jul 2024**, so a formal vodoprávní proceeding is now required. Older plants approved by ohlášení keep their approval — [cisteniodpadnichvod.cz](https://www.cisteniodpadnichvod.cz/a/legislativa-k-domovnim-cistickam-odpadnich-vod), [Ekocis](https://ekocis.cz/jak-postupovat-pri-povolovani-cisticky-odpadnich-vod-pro-rodinne-domy), [ABplast](https://www.abplast.cz/dotace-a-legislativa/legislativa-k-domovnim-cistirnam-odpadnich-vod-pruvodce-povolenim-krok-za-krokem)
- Permits are usually fixed-term, typically up to 10 years and extendable. Conditions usually require regular servicing and submission of sampling results — [ABplast](https://www.abplast.cz/dotace-a-legislativa/legislativa-k-domovnim-cistirnam-odpadnich-vod-pruvodce-povolenim-krok-za-krokem). One source says laboratory analyses are due **twice a year** — [JH projekty](https://jhprojekty.cz/sluzby/projekty-dcov/povoleni-dcov/)
- The discharge route matters. Infiltration into groundwater usually needs a hydrogeological assessment and stricter limits — [ABplast – co s přečištěnou vodou](https://www.abplast.cz/technologie-cov/co-delat-s-precistenou-vodou-z-cov). Connecting to a public sewer needs no separate discharge permit, but the sewer's rules apply — [Envi-pur](https://www.envi-pur.cz/cisticky-odpadnich-vod-jak-a-kde-ziskat-povoleni-k-cov/). Emission limits come from nařízení vlády 401/2015 and 57/2016 — [ABplast](https://www.abplast.cz/dotace-a-legislativa/legislativa-k-domovnim-cistirnam-odpadnich-vod-pruvodce-povolenim-krok-za-krokem)
- **Deadlines conflict:** one source gives 30 days for simple cases and 60 for complex ones — [ABplast](https://www.abplast.cz/dotace-a-legislativa/legislativa-k-domovnim-cistirnam-odpadnich-vod-pruvodce-povolenim-krok-za-krokem); another gives up to 3 months — [JH projekty](https://jhprojekty.cz/sluzby/projekty-dcov/povoleni-dcov/)
- MZe publishes Q&A for vodoprávní úřady (2024), which shows the practice is still settling under the new building act — [MZe – Dotazy vodoprávních úřadů 2024](https://mze.gov.cz/public/portal/mze/-a56132---SP6a0v6I/dotazy-a-diskuze-2024)

**Indicative container rental costs (Czech rental firms, ex VAT unless stated)**
- Obytná buňka, 6 m: from **3,300 Kč a month** for rentals up to 6 months and **3,100 Kč a month** for 6–12 months; longer terms are priced individually. The page is about 1.5 years old — [Bunky Praha](https://www.bunkypraha.cz/pronajem-obytnych-bunek-a-kontejneru/)

### Inferences
- **Utilities set the critical path.** A grid connection can take up to 11 months after the deposit, a ČOV permit now needs a full proceeding, and a camp may only be needed for 1–5 years. Together these create a clear service gap: **"utilities-as-a-service" for camps**. Examples are bridging power (generator, hybrid, or solar plus battery), sewage holding tanks with scheduled haulage or mobile ČOV with permits arranged in advance, and water supply with documented testing.
- **The sanitary module is the cost driver.** It costs about 3 times as much per month as a sleeping module, so the sanitary ratio chosen (Czech practice versus 1 per 15 under IFC) drives camp economics.
- **Recurring ČOV compliance.** Servicing visits, twice-yearly lab results and permit expiry are recurring compliance tasks to track, a fit for a building-records product.

- Not found: costs of water tanks or cisterns and the drinking-water testing duties for off-grid supply.

## 7. Operations pain points in worker camps (cleaning, laundry, catering, security, keys and access, damage disputes, check-in of rotating crews) and what is typically outsourced

### Takeaway
Sourced evidence here is thin; this section was planned for the final search round. What was found points to five recurring burdens:
- **identity and occupancy data** under frequent multi-agency inspections (about 200 police checks a month in one region);
- the **linen and cleaning regime** fixed in the KHS-approved provozní řád (at least every 14 days in the template);
- **equipment upkeep**: detectors in every room, ČOV servicing and sampling;
- **fees and wage deductions** that need written agreements;
- **reputational exposure** at scale, as the Dukovany "80-person block" story showed.

The IFC/EBRD note treats management, fee charging, security, grievance mechanisms and community relations as core camp processes. No source on typical outsourcing patterns in Czechia was retrieved.

### Cited Findings
- Police inspection intensity: 1,252 checks of accommodation facilities in one region in H1 2026 — [orlicky.net](https://www.orlicky.net/clanek.php?id_zpravy=12632122891786261006). The checks are multi-agency (OIP, finanční úřad, celní správa, ČSSZ, firefighters) — [krimi-plzen.cz](https://krimi-plzen.cz/a/cizina-2026-10-15-125504/), [Policie ČR](https://policie.gov.cz/clanek/uzemni-utvary-sprava-zapadoceskeho-kraje-zpravodajstvi-kontrola-pobytu-cizincu.aspx)
- The linen regime is part of the provozní řád (zacházení s prádlem). The KHS template sets changes after each guest and at least every 14 days — [HS Praha](https://www.hygpraha.cz/jake-povinnosti-musi-splnit-osoba-poskytujici-ubytovani-v-ubytovacich-zarizenich/), [KHS Plzeň template](https://www.khsplzen.cz/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/PR-ubytovaciho-zarizeni-aktualizace-2015-1.doc)
- The IFC/EBRD process areas are management and staff, charging fees, health and safety, security, workers' rights, grievance mechanisms and community relations — [IFC](https://www.ifc.org/en/insights-reports/2000/publications-gpn-workersaccommodation)
- Fees deducted from pay need written agreement and a known amount — [Dostupný advokát](https://dostupnyadvokat.cz/blog/co-muze-zamestnavatel-strhnout-z-platu)
- Scale and sensitivity: Dukovany II may need housing for up to 3,000–6,000 workers. A reported design of 80-person blocks without WC or showers drew public criticism; KHNP denies it — [iRozhlas](https://www.irozhlas.cz/zpravy-domov/6-tisic-novych-obyvatel-u-dukovan-kvuli-dostavbe-elektrarny-prijedou-do-regionu_2602101938_jos), [Blesk](https://www.blesk.cz/clanek/zpravy-politika/812145/experti-v-soku-z-korejskeho-navrhu-pro-ceske-delniky-v-dukovanech-dehonestujici-hodnoti-ho.html). Coverage of the Płock camp (about 6,000 workers) is headlined "může to být drsné" ("it can get rough") — [Ekonomický deník](https://ekonomickydenik.cz/jaderne-staveniste-dukovany-muze-to-byt-drsne-ukazuje-priklad-stavby-nove-rafinerie-v-polskem-plocku/)

### Inferences
These are labelled inferences, not sourced facts; they map the burdens to BULO modules.
- **Check-in of rotating crews:** passport capture, then Ubyport via the web service, domovní kniha, poplatek logic and bed assignment. This maps to BULO resident records.
- **Keys and access control:** key or card issue and return tied to the bed record, with a deposit or deduction on loss. This maps to BULO keys and payments.
- **Damage disputes:** photo inventories at check-in and check-out by room and bed, with defect tickets linked to the resident and to the dohoda o srážkách. This maps to BULO defect reporting and payments.
- **Cleaning and laundry:** schedules derived from the provozní řád, completion logs and evidence for KHS. This maps to BULO building records and tasks.
- **Safety equipment:** detector, extinguisher and ČOV maintenance calendars and logs. This maps to BULO building records.
- **Inspection readiness:** a one-click export of current occupants, IDs, report receipts, provozní řád, PBŘ and maintenance logs.
- **Grievance channel** in the IFC style, multilingual, logged and auditable. This is a differentiator for EPC and lender-financed projects.

### Gaps
- No retrieved source on which camp services (cleaning, laundry, catering, security) are usually outsourced in Czechia, on their unit costs, or on Czech camp-management providers. That search was blocked by the budget.
- No data on damage-dispute frequency, key-loss rates or crew-rotation patterns in Czech construction.

## 8. Synthesis: slow or burdensome steps, and repetitive compliance tasks a service or software company could remove or automate

### Takeaway
The slow steps happen **once per site**: siting under the územní plán and municipal consent, a permit that usually takes 3–5 months, a ČOV permit and a grid connection of up to 11 months. They suit a **service** offering: pre-approved module designs and permit packs, a bank of pre-cleared sites, and utilities-as-a-service.

The repetitive steps happen **per resident and per period**:
- Ubyport reporting within 3 working days, possibly 24 hours under eTurista;
- the domovní kniha and evidenční kniha with 6-year retention;
- poplatek z pobytu logic covering the 60-day rule and municipal exemptions;
- linen and cleaning logs under the provozní řád;
- detector and ČOV maintenance;
- deduction agreements and fee statements;
- readiness for frequent multi-agency inspections.

These suit **software**, and they overlap closely with BULO's existing modules (resident records, keys, defects, payments, building records).

### Cited Findings
- Permit: 30-day statutory decision for a jednoduchá stavba, but typically 3–5 months in practice — [Mobis](https://mobis.cz/projektova-dokumentace/pruvodce/jak-dlouho-trva-povoleni-stavby), [ČKAIT PIK 2026](https://zpravy.ckait.cz/media/pdf/pik-2026-delka-povolovani-staveb-v-cr.pdf)
- Siting: municipalities are amending územní plány against ubytovny — [Třebíčský deník](https://trebicsky.denik.cz/zpravy_region/dostavba-dukovan-ubytovny-delnici-obce-uzemni-plan-20260523.html)
- Grid: 50% deposit within 15 days; distributor deadline of 11 months in sample contracts — [zakonyprolidi 16/2016](https://www.zakonyprolidi.cz/cs/2016-16), [smlouvy.gov.cz sample](https://smlouvy.gov.cz/smlouva/soubor/9572075/Smlouva%20%C4%8CEZ%20Distribuce-anon.pdf)

- Ubyport: 3 working days; three submission modes including a web service — [MZV text](https://mzv.gov.cz/file/335740/Zakon_c._329_1999_Sb..pdf), [Policie ČR](https://policie.gov.cz/clanek/jake-jsou-moznosti-zasilani-dat-elektronicky-prostrednictvim-aplikace-ubyport.aspx). ### Inferences

- **Positioning for BULO.** The most defensible software wedge is a **"camp operating system"**. It would extend BULO's resident records, keys, defects and payments with Czech-specific compliance automation: Ubyport and eTurista, poplatek z pobytu, the provozní řád regime and inspection packs. It would also add IFC/EBRD-style auditability (density, sanitary ratios, grievances) for EPC buyers such as the Dukovany II supply chain.
- **Positioning for a service company.** The most valuable offer is to **take permitting and utilities risk off the contractor**: type-approved modules, pre-arranged utilities and siting support. The slow steps are one-off per site but sit on the critical path, and contractors cannot easily do them in-house.
- **Timing.** eTurista (2027–2028, with a 24-hour reporting draft) and the Dukovany II ramp-up (start 2029, peak 2030–2034) both make 2027–2029 a natural window to build and prove the product.
